Third-Party Vendor Risk Assessment, CloudDesk Solutions
Overview
This project demonstrates a practical Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) assessment for a fictional SaaS vendor being considered by a financial services organization.
The project simulates the work performed by a GRC analyst during vendor due diligence, including security questionnaire review, control assessment, evidence evaluation, risk assessment, gap analysis, remediation planning, and vendor approval.
The vendor used in this project is CloudDesk Solutions, a fictional cloud-based customer-support and workflow management provider.

Business Scenario
FinServe Financial Services is considering CloudDesk Solutions as a SaaS provider for customer-support and workflow management.
The proposed service may process:
Customer-support information
Customer contact information
Employee information
Internal business information
Operational records
Because CloudDesk would process potentially confidential information and support an important business function, FinServe requires a formal third-party risk assessment before unrestricted production use.

Project Objectives
The objectives of this assessment were to:
Profile the proposed vendor.
Conduct vendor security due diligence.
Evaluate vendor security controls.
Review supporting evidence.
Identify control gaps.
Assess vendor risks.
Develop a remediation plan.
Determine an overall vendor risk rating.
Produce an executive-level vendor risk report.
Provide a documented vendor approval recommendation.

Key Findings
The assessment identified eight primary vendor risks involving:
Data retention
Access reviews
Vulnerability assurance
Penetration testing
Disaster recovery
Subprocessor management
Security monitoring
Independent security assurance
The overall vendor risk rating was:
HIGH

Vendor Decision
The recommended vendor status is:
CONDITIONAL APPROVAL PENDING REMEDIATION
CloudDesk may continue through the onboarding process subject to remediation, evidence collection, contractual requirements, and appropriate risk oversight.
Unrestricted production deployment should not occur until required high-priority findings have been addressed or formally accepted.

Risk Methodology
Risks were evaluated using:
Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact
Likelihood:
1 = Low
2 = Medium
3 = High
Impact:
1 = Low
2 = Medium
3 = High
The project uses a risk-based approach to prioritize vendor findings and remediation activities.

Key GRC Concepts Demonstrated
Due Diligence
Reviewing a vendor's security practices before onboarding.
Evidence-Based Assessment
Evaluating documentation and evidence rather than relying only on vendor statements.
Risk-Based Decision Making
Using identified risks and control weaknesses to determine the appropriate vendor status.
Remediation
Requiring the vendor to address identified control gaps.
Residual Risk
Recognizing that some third-party risk remains even after controls are implemented.
Continuous Monitoring
Recognizing that vendor risk management is an ongoing process rather than a one-time assessment.
